Arte de la Montaña
Gallery About Order

Arte de la Montaña is the personal studio of Andrea “Pelusa” Montaña, a painter working across acrylic, oil, pen, pencil, alcohol markers and pastel.

Her work has been admitted into prestigious institutions, such as Parsons School of Design. Her work was influenced and mentored under Lina Sinisterra, a top 50 recognized CollegeBoard multimedia artist.

The catalogue gathers studies and finished pieces made between 2020 and 2026; from quick pen sketches of urban life to large acrylic canvases of cityscapes. She uses a quite childish and immature expression in her paintings.

Every piece in this gallery is real, painted by hand, and lives in the studio until it finds a home. Use the Order page to enquire about any of them.

Influences

From childhood readings of Mafalda to long walks through Andean towns, the work draws on travel, family stories and the textures of paper, canvas and ceramic. Animal subjects recur — the sloth, the ladybug, the Andean bear that gives this site its mark.

A note on craft — every sticker, button and stencil in this catalogue (including the marks scattered through this page) is hand-drawn before it is scanned in. Even the navigation here uses no SVG icons, just the studio's own ink.
